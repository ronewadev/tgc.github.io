About Techno Globe Center

Techno Globe Center SA (Pty) Ltd is an ICT consulting company which specializes on Microsoft Solution packages, from various business needs and requirements. Thus the company caters for various organizational structures be private, public or NGOs from small, medium to large scales within the market. At TGC SA we are determined and committed to provide our customers with Innovative, Fresh and Compatible Technological Solution packages that can meet & exceed our clients’ business expectations.

At TGC SA we believe in change, fresh ideas, youth empowerment, building business opportunities and customer relationships through a transparency of integrity, excellence and innovation. By always making sure that we focus & improve on service delivery and customer service qualities in going the extra-mile to achieve our client’s main objective and organizational needs, as we understand the importance of their business needs and struggles.

TGC SA was established in 2013 by black young South Africans, thus the company is 100% black owned and registered in South Africa at CIPRO. The company has a group of young black, energetic & skilful individuals with different sets of skills, knowledge and work experience backgrounds, whereby those skills vary from design, development, administration, monitoring, auditing, analysis, project management and more. All this coming from a company that is young, dynamic & passionate about its service & the product which it delivers to its clients with pride & achievement from the brand.

Our Mission

Our Mission is to become one of the many leading ICT companies within Africa including international, as we focus on igniting and pioneering excellence on Service Delivery & Quality. Being true to our core business values by adding value to our client's business and delivering optimized quality work with cost-effective solutions.

Our Vision

Our Vision is to be inspired and recognized by our employees, customers, shareholders and partners as being one of the best young dynamic & innovative ICT organization in South Africa.
